Maintaining Project Context with AI · Product Decisions

How the findings changed the build

The experiment changed what seemed worth building. I kept the parts that made uncertainty and new evidence easier to handle without making people manage the underlying structure themselves.

My role / AI’s role

My role: I decided to keep pursuing the narrower problem after the original hypothesis weakened, challenged the architecture and alternatives, made the scope and product calls, and drove the interaction, UI, and behavioral QA that simplified the prototype.

AI’s role: Claude substantially designed the underlying review and state-update architecture, including how evidence stays separate from current understanding, when changes need review, and how pending evidence resurfaces, and handled most of the technical implementation.

Working question

The prototype became a product experiment, not proof of the architecture.

Once the broad hypothesis weakened, I narrowed the build around one question: What should a system do when new information challenges something the project is already relying on?

01
FindingSome questions are genuinely unresolved.

The automation percentage had not been established; a useful-sounding number would make an unknown look settled.

Product decision“Not established” is a first-class answer.

The boundary stays visible so the user can decide what evidence is needed next.

02
FindingNew evidence can weaken an old assumption without supplying its replacement.

Account-level access exceptions made standard plan rules unsafe to treat as sufficient, but did not establish the authoritative source.

Product decisionNew evidence does not automatically become what the project treats as true.

Review separates what the evidence establishes from what it does not establish before later work starts relying on a changed answer.

03
FindingSome updates need more review than others.

The important moment is when a proposed change would alter something people or AI will rely on later.

Product decisionHuman review belongs where an important change would alter what the project relies on.

Supporting details can stay lightweight. Open questions and ideas should not quietly become project facts.

04
Workflow riskA review queue only works if somebody remembers to clean it up.

Perfect knowledge-management hygiene is not a realistic product assumption.

Product decisionRelevant pending evidence resurfaces where work happens.

Meeting Prep can flag that a pending review may affect the briefing while still letting the user continue from the current reviewed understanding.

What the prototype is maintaining

The project’s current reviewed understanding sits between activity and downstream work.

The prototype is not asking AI to decide what the project believes. New activity can become evidence and suggest a change, but important changes pass through human review before they alter the current understanding that people or AI work from.

Restraint

What I deliberately did not optimize for.

The goal was to build enough to test the state and interaction model, not to maximize the amount of AI technology in the demo.

No silent replacement truth

New evidence stays separate from what the project currently treats as true until an important change is reviewed.

No unnecessary AI machinery

For this stage, fixed test data was enough to test the interaction.

No warning overload

Pending evidence is surfaced contextually where it matters rather than turning every screen into a wall of caution.

The unresolved product betState trades reconstruction risk for maintenance risk.

Keeping project state explicit can reduce guesswork, but it creates another risk: that state can get stale while still looking reliable. State only works if it can identify the important changes that actually need review without creating more maintenance than the problem is worth. A real version would also need a way to track, correct, and undo bad updates.

The system shouldn't depend on perfect knowledge-management habits to keep consequential project state current.
