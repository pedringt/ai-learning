02 / cards

Five questions before choosing controls

Data

What enters the model, and who may retain or train on it?

Authority

Can AI only suggest, or can it send, spend, approve, or change records?

Impact

What if the output is wrong, stale, biased, or leaked?

Access

Could retrieval expose information across users or tenants?

Oversight

Can a person inspect, stop, challenge, and recover?

When to stop and who must approve

Stop / escalate triggers

TriggerRequired response
Sensitive or regulated data newly involvedPause scope change; route to data, legal, privacy and security owners
New write or external permissionsReassess authority and approvals before enabling sends, financial actions, records or production changes
Consequential AI recommendationDetermine whether a human has meaningful review and accountable ownership
Unknown retention, training or vendor termsResolve provider/data-processing requirements before use
No clear logs, accountability or reversalBlock consequential rollout until controls and incident owner exist
Framework anchors: Use NIST AI Risk Management Framework (Govern, Map, Measure, Manage), the NIST Generative AI Profile, and ISO/IEC 42001 as orientation aids where applicable. These do not replace organizational policy, contracts, legal advice, or specialist privacy/security/compliance review.
Re-review events: Repeat the risk review when data sources, audience, model/provider, tool scopes, autonomy, business impact or retention rules materially change. Risk classification is contextual.
PRACTICAL FRAMEWORK / AI PRODUCT WORK

Untrusted retrieval and prompt injection

Retrieved pages, documents, email and tool results are data, not authority. They may include hostile or mistaken instructions.

Boundary testExpected safe behavior
Document says “ignore prior rules”Treat as source text; do not alter system rules, tool access or approval requirements.
Cross-tenant or revoked source appearsEnforce source-level permissions before retrieval and again before disclosure.
Source asks for secrets or external transferReject unauthorized action, minimize sensitive data in logs, preserve trace for approved review.
Permission, policy or source provenance is missingFail closed for consequential actions; ask for approved access or escalate.
Product owner question: Which trusted component enforces the boundary if the model follows the malicious text?
