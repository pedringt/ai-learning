PM cheat sheet: turn policy into product decisions and controls

USEFUL DEFAULT FOR CONSEQUENTIAL WORKFLOWS

AI interprets→software enforces→people authorize

Use stricter controls as data, authority, or impact rises.

1 · FAST RISK SCAN

Ask these before choosing controls

3 · CONTROL MENU

Choose controls that match the risk

DATA

Minimize/redact inputs; approved enterprise tools;

retention/training settings; vendor/DPA review.

Data: What enters the model? Client-confidential, personal,

regulated, credentials, or proprietary information?

ACCESS

Least privilege; tenant/project boundaries; separate

read from write permissions; secrets outside prompts.

Authority: Does AI only suggest, or can it decide, send,

publish, approve, change records, spend money, or touch

production?

Impact: What happens if the answer is wrong, biased, stale,

leaked, or confidently unsupported?

HUMAN

Human approval before consequential actions; clear

escalation path; do not hide uncertainty behind

confidence scores.

Access: Who may see the source data and output? Could

retrieval expose information across users, teams, or clients?

EVIDENCE

Ground important claims in approved sources; show

provenance when users need to verify what the AI

relied on.

Oversight: Can a person review important outputs, inspect

evidence/logs, stop the system, and recover from a bad

action?

TEST

Representative + high-risk evals;

permission-boundary tests;

prompt-injection/adversarial cases; safe-failure

behavior.

OBSERVE

Logs/traces appropriate to the risk; monitor failures,

corrections, drift, model/vendor changes, and misuse.

2 · PRACTICAL RISK LEVELS

Scale review to the use case

RECOVER

Pause/kill switch for risky actions; rollback where

possible; preserve logs; incident owner + notification

path.

LOW

Drafting, brainstorming, public-info summaries. No

sensitive data; no consequential action.

Normal QA + approved-tool rules.

4 · PM REVIEW FLOW

1 Intake→2 Classify risk→

MEDIUM Internal knowledge, client-confidential content in

approved systems, support suggestions, code

assistance.

3 Data/privacy + vendor review→

4 Define authority & controls→5 Eval/test→6 Approve→

Privacy/security review, permissions, evals, source

checks, monitoring.

7 Monitor→8 Incident/review

The PM owns the product questions; specialists own legal/security

determinations.

HIGH

Sensitive/regulated data + material decisions,

autonomous external actions, production changes,

employment/financial/legal/medical impact.

Re-review when data, permissions, model/vendor, or impact

meaningfully changes.

Formal approval, human authorization, least

privilege, strong evals, logging, rollback/kill switch,

incident plan.

Risk is contextual. Company policy, contracts, and law can raise the

required level.

STOP AND ESCALATE WHEN

A new use case introduces sensitive/regulated data or unclear

data-retention/training terms.

AI output becomes a material input to a consequential

decision without meaningful human review.

An agent gains new write/action permissions, especially

money, production, customer messages, or record changes.

You cannot explain who is accountable, what was accessed,

why an action happened, or how to undo it.
