REFERENCE DETAIL / RESTORED

Do less before scaling up

Practical escalation: Start with a known signal and the smallest sufficient context; expand to likely sources, adjacent evidence and only then broader investigation. Use a fast capable model for ordinary tasks and a reasoning model only when ambiguity merits it. Agents need explicit tool scopes, stop conditions and escalation.
Measure total outcome cost: Include tool calls, latency, retries, cleanup, human review and bad-decision risk. Cheap tokens do not imply a cheap successful outcome.
PRACTICAL FRAMEWORK / AI PRODUCT WORK

Cost per successful outcome and spending authority

Do not optimize tokens alone. Track model calls, paid APIs, tool and compute charges, retries, review time, cleanup, support and downstream correction costs.

Measure or controlDecision rule
Cost per successful outcomeTotal operating + human cost ÷ verified completed tasks; include failures in total cost.
Per-action and total budgetsHard limits enforced by software, not by a reminder in a prompt.
Allowed services and escalationApproved spend categories, approval above thresholds and transaction logs.
Stop conditionTerminate repeated calls, loops or spending when the outcome stops improving.
