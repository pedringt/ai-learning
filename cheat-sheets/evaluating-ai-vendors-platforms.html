05 / list

POC rules

  • Set acceptance criteria before demonstrations
  • Use the same realistic sources and high-risk cases
  • Record missing features instead of changing the test
  • Count human review and support costs
  • Plan replacement if terms, models or pricing change
REFERENCE DETAIL / RESTORED

Make the vendor tradeoff visible

Vendor A vs Vendor B: Vendor A is cheaper per call. Vendor B has similar measured accuracy but stronger audit logs and escalation support. For low-risk drafting, A may offer better value. For consequential record changes, B’s controls and support may justify the premium. The best model is not necessarily the best vendor or product.

Fair comparison checklist

DimensionRequire comparable proof
Workflow and edge casesSame real tasks, source inputs, acceptance bar and failure conditions
Authority and privacyData use, retention, subprocessors, user scopes, audit, deletion and contractual commitments
OperationsUptime/support terms, incident notification, region availability, human escalation
Cost per successful outcomeLicenses, model usage, implementation, integration, review, cleanup and support
PortabilityExport formats, ownership of data/prompts, vendor-model switching and exit plan
Do not move the goalposts: Write the quality and risk acceptance thresholds before seeing a polished demo. Record unsupported requirements; compare real workflow improvement and operational burden, not marketing-feature counts.
