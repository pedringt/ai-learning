Q-103 failure

Agent found the correct open Review but stated ownership as accepted fact. The revised instruction made it say ownership remained under review, then the same case was rerun.
05 / list

Transferable lessons

  • Read-only tools do not prevent unsafe interpretation
  • Trace pinpoints the first observable wrong layer
  • Retest the same case after changing instructions
  • Separate safe uncertainty from excessive review burden
  • Never confuse controlled demos with production evidence

Q-103: the precise authority failure

Read the source hierarchy

Source stateWhat may be asserted
Current State says training ownership under reviewOwnership is not established
Priya offers to helpAn offer, not an appointment
Open Review proposes PriyaA pending proposal, not group approval
Later approved updateOnly after the authorized transition may accepted ownership change
Fix and retest: In the controlled Q-103 exercise, the agent retrieved the right evidence and open Review but stated Priya owned customer-support training as established fact. The targeted instruction clarified that open Reviews must be called pending/proposed unless Current State independently supports the fact. Rerunning the same case correctly kept ownership unresolved and pointed the human to the Review.
Boundary of the exercise: This was a controlled read-only State Question Investigator exercise, not proof of production robustness. Retrieval success is not authority correctness; trace the first synthesis error, preserve chronology and evidence provenance, and monitor whether excessive caution creates review burden.
