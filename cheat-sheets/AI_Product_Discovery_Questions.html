Three starting lenses

Walk the real path from trigger to outcome, not the official process diagram.

Pain

Find delay, errors, rework, handoffs, workarounds, and expert judgment.

Baseline

Quantify volume, elapsed time, effort, escalation, and throughput.

Questions that change the decision

AskWhy it matters
How often does this happen?Scale, priority, and likely automation value.
Who handles it, and how do roles differ?Different workflows hidden under one name.
What happens when normal steps fail?Exceptions and handoff burden.
What information must people have?Retrieval, data readiness, and permissions.
What would better look like?An outcome measurable against the baseline.

06 / list

Discovery note template

  • Workflow: trigger → steps → handoffs → outcome
  • Pain: where, for whom, how often, at what cost
  • Baseline: time, volume, quality, rework
  • Constraints: data, systems, permissions, policy
  • Unknowns and smallest next evidence-gathering action

Questions worth restoring

AskWhat it reveals
Where do experienced people handle this differently from newer people?Tacit expertise, hidden decisions, training gaps and variations in judgment
What happens when the normal path fails?Actual exception volume, rework, escalations and manual fallbacks
Which side channels, spreadsheets or memories do people rely on?The real workflow, not only the official process map
What information is required at each handoff?Missing inputs, permissions, freshness and system-of-record boundaries
Discovery warning: Stakeholders may describe an ideal process while the work actually runs through shared spreadsheets, informal chats, personal judgment and exceptions. Observe real cases from trigger through outcome, including how newer users differ from experts.
PRACTICAL FRAMEWORK / AI PRODUCT WORK

When faster AI moves the bottleneck

WorkflowBefore AIAfter a faster drafting step
Support responseAgents spend time drafting replies.Drafting shrinks, but correction, source verification or supervisor approval queues grow.
What to measureDrafting time and response throughput.Total resolution time, correction burden, escalations, repeat contacts and customer outcome.
Discovery question: If this step became ten times faster, where would work pile up next? Test the end-to-end workflow, not just local speed.
