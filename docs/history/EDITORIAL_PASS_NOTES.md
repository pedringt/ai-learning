# Natural-voice editorial pass

Date: 2026-09-03

This pass reviewed the public portfolio and State prototype copy for language that could feel templated or AI-generated.

## What changed

- Removed visible em dashes from public-facing prose and demo content.
- Simplified several symmetrical contrast constructions and overly polished abstractions.
- Split a few long sentences that were carrying too many ideas.
- Reduced repeated explanation after a conclusion was already clear.
- Made the Legal AI verdict more direct and corrected two comma splices.
- Made the Meridian conclusion more candid and conversational while preserving its limitation.
- Tightened the AI Search explanation and State case-study opening.
- Replaced meeting-note placeholders with clearer prompts for the user.

## What stayed unchanged

- Product claims, evidence, metrics, attribution, and caveats.
- Portfolio strategy and page hierarchy.
- State's authority model and settled product language.
- Layout, CSS, navigation, and application behavior.
- Technical punctuation used for arrows, date labels, and interface structure where it is functional rather than prose styling.

The edits are intentionally restrained. The goal was to sound natural and professional, not to flatten the writing or hide the fact that AI contributed substantially to the work.
