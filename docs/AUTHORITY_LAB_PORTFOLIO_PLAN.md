# Authority Lab portfolio integration plan

Draft planning notes for when Authority Lab is ready to join the public portfolio.

## Current direction

Authority Lab should be treated as a major portfolio project, not just another supporting artifact.

The current portfolio story is strongest when the major projects each answer a different product question:

- **State** — What is true right now, and what still needs a person to confirm?
- **Authority Lab** — How much of this workflow should run without human review?
- **Project Health** — What needs attention, and what does the signal actually mean?
- **Tastemake** — Can users see and correct what the model learns about them?

The broader thread across the portfolio is practical AI product work where evidence, boundaries, uncertainty, and human judgment are explicit.

Do not reposition the whole portfolio as "AI governance." Governance is one important part of the work, but not the umbrella for everything.

## Homepage

Preferred direction when Authority Lab goes live:

1. **State** remains the flagship / largest project.
2. **Authority Lab** becomes a homepage featured project.
3. **Project Health** remains a homepage featured project.
4. **Tastemake** moves off the homepage but remains Featured in Applied Work.
5. Keep a clear **See all applied work** path rather than turning the homepage into a project directory.

The homepage should likely feature these three projects:

- State
- Authority Lab
- Project Health

This trio communicates a coherent product story without making the homepage too dense.

Before release, review homepage copy for whether it still reflects the current body of work. The language should acknowledge the recurring themes of evidence, boundaries, uncertainty, human review, and product judgment without making the site sound like a governance-only portfolio.

## Applied Work

Preferred Featured order:

1. State
2. Authority Lab
3. Project Health
4. Tastemake

A clean 2x2 Featured grid is the preferred starting point.

Authority Lab should be placed intentionally near the top, not appended as "the newest project."

Supporting Work should remain flexible enough for future additions rather than assuming the current set is permanent.

## Future work to keep in mind

### NARC

NARC may not be public on the site for a while.

Do not reserve homepage space for it now, but avoid an Applied Work structure that makes adding another supporting or experimental project awkward later.

Likely role if/when it returns:
- experimental / interactive AI concept work
- supporting evidence of product thinking rather than a current homepage anchor

### Larger AI governance exercise / case study

A broader governance exercise is in progress and should remain distinct from Authority Lab.

Working distinction:

- **Authority Lab** — applied product build focused on deciding when a capability is ready for more autonomy, enforcing approved boundaries, and reducing autonomy when conditions change.
- **Governance exercise / case study** — broader organizational and product-governance thinking: policies, decision rights, risk tiers, review processes, ownership, escalation, evaluation expectations, and operating model.

Do not let Authority Lab absorb all governance positioning.

The two pieces should show different levels of work:
- Authority Lab = governance inside a product / workflow
- Governance case study = governance across a team or organization

If the governance case study becomes substantial enough, revisit whether it belongs in Featured Applied Work.

## Learning Library / Professional Edge / Governance Handbook

When Authority Lab goes live, review learning/reference pages for concepts that are no longer only "things learned" but now have applied proof.

Likely candidates:
- reversible autonomy
- human authorization
- evaluation before expansion
- software enforcement
- precommitted criteria
- adversarial testing
- separation of model recommendation from permission

Where useful, link to Authority Lab as the applied example rather than repeating the concept in multiple places.

For the AI Governance Handbook, keep the distinction clear:
- handbook = reference / learning material
- Authority Lab = applied product work

Light cross-links such as **See this applied in Authority Lab** are preferable to expanding the handbook to duplicate the case study.

## Cross-project copy audit before release

Before Authority Lab is promoted, review major project cards and case-study intros for overlap.

Each flagship project should own a distinct question rather than making broad claims that compete with the others.

Also audit for repeated governance language so Authority Lab becomes the concrete applied proof instead of having the same story scattered across the portfolio.

## Case-study consistency

Authority Lab should use the same case-study family as the existing pages:
- same hero structure
- same meta block
- same In short treatment
- same top CTA pattern
- same kicker + H2 rhythm
- same My Role section
- same Where I Landed ending
- Back to Applied Work as the only bottom action

Keep Authority Lab's five-step product loop as its main distinctive visual element.

Reduce extra card grids where they make the page denser than the other case studies, especially Lingering Questions.

## Promotion gate

These are planning notes only.

Do not update the homepage, Applied Work, Learning Library, Professional Edge, Governance Handbook, or any production/staging page until the Authority Lab product redesign and case study are settled and Paige explicitly approves implementation and the destination.
