About Project Health

How Project Health works

Project Health summarizes operational evidence across delivery, quality, site analytics, and infrastructure without replacing the underlying systems.

Signals

GitHub, Vercel, Render, Neon, project-specific quality checks, and aggregate site analytics.

Software decides

What data to fetch, what counts as a failure, and which evidence the investigator may access.

AI helps

On request, it explains selected failures when deterministic signals alone are not enough.

Human decides

A person verifies the diagnosis and chooses whether code, configuration, or deployment should change.

Quality is project-specific. State measures interpretation and grounded answers. Tastemake checks recommendation grounding and calibration. NARC relies mostly on deterministic gameplay tests plus human playtesting.

Missing telemetry stays missing rather than being guessed. Aggregate site analytics never include project content.

Role & attribution

This is an applied AI product exercise, not a claim that the implementation was written by hand.
