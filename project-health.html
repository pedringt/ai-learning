About this dashboard

How Project Health works

I built this after managing several live projects made it harder to tell what actually needed attention across delivery, quality, usage, and infrastructure. The dashboard keeps deterministic monitoring separate from AI interpretation and leaves consequential decisions with a person.

Signals

GitHub, Vercel, Render, Neon, product-specific quality checks, and aggregate usage.

Software decides

What data to fetch, what counts as a failure, and which evidence the investigator may access.

AI helps

On request, it explains selected failures when deterministic signals alone are not enough.

Human decides

A person verifies the diagnosis and chooses whether any code, configuration, or deployment should change.

Quality is project-specific. State measures interpretation and grounded answers. Tastemake checks recommendation grounding and calibration. NARC relies mostly on deterministic gameplay tests plus human playtesting.

Role & attribution

This is an applied AI product exercise, not a claim that the implementation was written by hand.

My work

Product framing, workflow design, guardrails, quality model, failure behavior, QA, and iteration.

AI-assisted work

Implementation support, code generation, research support, and the bounded failure-investigation summary.

Project Health summarizes operational evidence; it does not replace the underlying systems. Missing telemetry stays missing rather than being guessed. Aggregate analytics never include project content.
