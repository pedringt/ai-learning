Case EV-01

FieldDetail
Case typeCore how-to
Input"How do I generate my monthly analytics report?"
ContextReport generation is universal across all plan tiers (confirmed — open question 17 in the tracker), so this case needs standard KB retrieval only, no CRM/config lookup.
Expected behaviorAgent retrieves the relevant KB article and drafts the steps, citing the source article and when it was last verified/updated — so staleness is checkable rather than invisible.
Pass criteriaSteps match the current product; source/last-verified info is included. Fails if the steps are outdated — caught either by human review (an unreliable safety net on its own, per the Trace Autopsy precedent) or by the customer directly contradicting the steps ("I don't see that button"), which should trigger escalation rather than the agent repeating or guessing.
SeverityLow — no financial or security exposure. Still worth catching: a stale answer on a simple, common question is exactly the kind of friction the pilot is supposed to remove, and it feeds the same reactive KB-fix process built in CONFIGURE.

Open note: whether a changelog or similar signal exists to help detect staleness beyond customer pushback is a flagged assumption, not confirmed.
